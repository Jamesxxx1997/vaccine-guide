## (a) 對某疫苗曾有 anaphylaxis → 其他疫苗怎麼處理（成分導向評估、皮膚測試、分次給予、轉介）

**引句 1（最關鍵）**：
> "Summary Statement 7: Patients who have had an apparent anaphylactic reaction after immunization should undergo immediate-type allergy skin testing to help confirm that the reaction was IgE mediated and determine the responsible component of the vaccine. (B)"

出處：AAAAI_ACAAI疫苗過敏practice_parameter_2012.pdf，PDF 第6頁（期刊印刷頁碼 J Allergy Clin Immunol 2012;130:**30**）
中文整理：曾對疫苗有疑似立即型過敏性休克反應者，應接受立即型過敏皮膚測試，以確認是否為 IgE 媒介反應並找出可疑成分（證據等級 B）。

**引句 2**：
> "Summary Statement 9: In a patient with a history and skin test results consistent with an IgE-mediated reaction to a vaccine who requires additional doses of the suspect vaccine or other vaccines with common ingredients, consideration can be given to administering the vaccine in graded doses under observation. (C)"

出處：AAAAI_ACAAI疫苗過敏practice_parameter_2012.pdf，PDF 第9頁（印刷頁碼 **33**）
中文整理：若病史與皮膚測試結果一致支持 IgE 媒介反應，而病人仍需接種可疑疫苗或含相同成分之其他疫苗，**可考慮**在監測下採分次漸增劑量方式給予（證據等級 C）——此為使用者要求的「可考慮」標示依據之一。

**引句 3（COVID-19 mRNA 疫苗，2020s 更新版，證據等級更高）**：
> "Recommendation 3: We recommend that individuals who had an immediate allergic reaction of any severity to the 1st dose mRNA COVID-19 vaccine can receive additional mRNA COVID-19 vaccine doses, and those who have a history of an allergic reaction of any severity to one of the vaccine excipients can receive either their initial or additional mRNA COVID-19 vaccine doses." （Strong Recommendation; Moderate Certainty of Evidence）

出處：AAAAI_ACAAI_COVID疫苗過敏GRADE共識更新_2023.pdf，PDF 第9頁
中文整理：對第一劑 mRNA COVID-19 疫苗曾有任何嚴重度立即型過敏反應者，**強烈建議**可接受後續劑次；對疫苗賦形劑曾有過敏史者，亦可接種初始或後續劑次——這是「可考慮／可接種」的高證據等級依據。

**引句 4（不建議額外皮膚測試預測風險）**：
> "Recommendation 2: ...we recommend against vaccine or vaccine excipient testing prior to initial mRNA COVID-19 vaccination in an attempt to predict the rare individual who will have a severe allergic reaction to an initial vaccine dose."

出處：AAAAI_ACAAI_COVID疫苗過敏GRADE共識更新_2023.pdf，PDF 第7頁
中文整理：對「初次接種前、無過敏史」者，**不建議**用疫苗或賦形劑測試來預測是否會發生嚴重過敏反應（此點與 2012 年版對「已發生反應後」建議做皮膚測試不同，須注意情境差異：測試是用在「反應後」找成分，不是用在「反應前」篩檢）。

**引句 5（轉介過敏科）**：
> "In a patient with a history and skin test results consistent with an IgE-mediated reaction to a vaccine who requires additional doses of the suspect vaccine or other vaccines with common ingredients... Referral of the individual to an allergist for evaluation is usually indicated to possibly determine the component responsible, before making decisions regarding administration of the additional doses of the same vaccine or other vaccines that have the same components."

出處：CDC 通則 General Best Practice Guidelines for Immunization.pdf，PDF 第76頁（printed 76）
中文整理：轉介過敏科評估，釐清可疑成分後，再決定是否可接種相同或含相同成分之其他疫苗。

## (b) 蛋過敏：流感、黃熱病、MMR、狂犬病各怎麼說

**引句 1（流感，ACIP 2025-26 最新版立場）**：
> "ACIP recommends that all persons aged ≥6 months with egg allergy should receive influenza vaccine and that any influenza vaccine (egg based or non–egg based) that is otherwise appropriate for the recipient's age and health status can be used" ... "Egg allergy alone necessitates no additional safety measures for influenza vaccination beyond those recommended for any recipient of any vaccine, regardless of severity of previous reaction to egg."

出處：ACIP流感疫苗建議_MMWR2025-26.pdf，PDF 第3–4頁（已用 `pdftotext -f 3 -l 4` 單頁抽取回查確認）
中文整理：ACIP 現行（2025-26季）立場——所有 ≥6 個月大的蛋過敏者皆可接種任何年齡層合適的流感疫苗（蛋製或非蛋製皆可），單純蛋過敏不需任何額外防護措施，無論過去對蛋的反應嚴重度為何。

**引句 2（MMR，蛋過敏已非禁忌）**：
> "Thus, egg allergy is no longer considered a contra-indication to the administration of MMR vaccine and recipients need not be screened for egg allergy [7, 132]. As described below, most anaphylactic reactions to the MMR vaccine have been attributed to gelatin allergy."

出處：WAO_ICON國際共識_疫苗過敏反應_2016.pdf，PDF 第14頁（文件自身頁碼 Page 14 of 21）
中文整理：蛋過敏已不再是 MMR 疫苗的接種禁忌，接種前也不需篩檢蛋過敏；MMR 疫苗發生過敏性休克絕大多數其實是明膠過敏所致，**不是蛋過敏**——這是常見的臨床誤解，需特別標註區分。

**引句 3（黃熱病，仍需專科評估）**：
> "Yellow fever vaccine is prepared in chicken embryos [144] and contains measurable amounts of ovalbumin [129]. The vaccine may also contain chicken proteins [145]. Anaphylactic reactions have been reported after receipt of yellow fever vaccine, but the egg allergy status of these patients is not known [146]. There are no studies evaluating the administration of yellow fever vaccine as a single dose in the usual manner to egg-allergic recipients and thus it is unknown whether or not this would induce allergic reactions. The package insert describes a protocol for vaccine skin testing in egg-allergic or chicken-allergic recipients [144]."

出處：WAO_ICON國際共識_疫苗過敏反應_2016.pdf，PDF 第14頁
中文整理：黃熱病疫苗以雞胚培養、含可測得的卵白蛋白，且無研究證實蛋過敏者可直接以常規方式接種——**故仍建議依仿單所述先做皮膚測試**，陰性才以常規方式接種，陽性則採分次給予（見下引句）。此點與流感/MMR 明顯不同，禁忌／建議標示不可類推。

**引句 4（黃熱病皮膚測試流程，接續上句）**：
> "A prick skin test is performed with the vaccine diluted 1:10, and if negative, an intradermal skin test is performed with the vaccine diluted 1:100. If these skin tests are negative, the vaccine can be administered in the usual manner. If the skin tests are positive, the vaccine can be administered in graded doses under observation..."

出處：WAO_ICON國際共識_疫苗過敏反應_2016.pdf，PDF 第14頁
中文整理：黃熱病疫苗针对蛋過敏/雞肉過敏者的仿單建議流程——prick test 1:10 陰性才做 intradermal 1:100，兩者皆陰性可常規接種；陽性則需在監測下分次給予。

**引句 5（狂犬病疫苗，非蛋過敏相關但同屬培養基來源過敏原）**：
> "From October 1997 through December 2005, the Vaccine Adverse Event Reporting System (VAERS) received 336 reports of AEFIs to the purified chick embryo Cell (PCEC, RabAvert) vaccine, 20 of which were classified as serious, following vaccination in the U.S. Of the 20 serious AEFIs, three were classified as possible anaphylaxis."

出處：WAO_ICON國際共識_疫苗過敏反應_2016.pdf，PDF 第9頁（原文件段落標題 "Rabies vaccine"）
中文整理：狂犬病純化雞胚細胞疫苗（PCEC/RabAvert）1997–2005年美國通報336例不良事件，20例嚴重，其中3例疑似過敏性休克——文件未提供明確「蛋過敏者可否接種」的分級建議，僅提供發生率背景資料，此點應誠實標示為資料有限、非明確可考慮/不建議之分級陳述。

## (c) 明膠、乳膠、neomycin／抗生素、酵母、PEG／polysorbate 各怎麼說

**引句 1（明膠，需轉介評估）**：
> "Persons who have had an anaphylactic reaction to gelatin or gelatin-containing products should be evaluated by an allergist prior to receiving gelatin-containing vaccines"

出處：CDC 通則 General Best Practice Guidelines for Immunization.pdf，PDF 第78頁（printed 78）
中文整理：曾對明膠或含明膠製品發生過敏性休克者，接種含明膠疫苗前應先轉介過敏科評估。

**引句 2（乳膠，依嚴重度分流）**：
> "If a person reports a severe anaphylactic allergy to latex, vaccines supplied in vials or syringes that contain natural rubber latex should be avoided if possible... For patients with a history of contact allergy to latex, vaccines supplied in vials or syringes that contain dry natural rubber or natural rubber latex may be administered."

出處：CDC_PinkBook附錄B_疫苗包裝乳膠含量表_2022.pdf（同段文字亦見於 CDC 通則 General Best Practice Guidelines，PDF 第80頁 printed 80）
中文整理：對乳膠有嚴重過敏性休克病史者，應盡量避開含天然乳膠瓶塞／針筒之疫苗；單純接觸性過敏（非立即型）則可正常接種——**嚴重度是分流的關鍵**，不可一概而論為「乳膠過敏＝不能打」。

**引句 3（neomycin，遲發型非禁忌、立即型需評估）**：
> "A history of delayed-type reactions to neomycin is not a contraindication for administration of neomycin-containing vaccines. There has only been 1 reported case of immediate hypersensitivity reaction following a neomycin-containing vaccine (27). Persons who have had anaphylactic reactions to neomycin should be [evaluated by an allergist prior to receiving vaccines containing neomycin]"

出處：CDC 通則 General Best Practice Guidelines for Immunization.pdf，PDF 第79頁（printed 79）
中文整理：遲發型（接觸性皮膚炎型）新黴素過敏**不是**接種含新黴素疫苗的禁忌；僅有1例立即型過敏性反應報告；曾對新黴素有過敏性休克者，接種前應先由過敏科評估。

**引句 4（酵母，B型肝炎疫苗相關）**：
> "Hepatitis B: Hepatitis B, Yeast" [as skin-testing components] / in-vitro IgE testing: "Yeast"

出處：WAO_ICON國際共識_疫苗過敏反應_2016.pdf，PDF 第13頁（Table 4: Examples of testing used to assess specific vaccines suspected of causing allergic reactions）
中文整理：懷疑對B型肝炎疫苗過敏者，評估項目包含疫苗本身及酵母（B肝疫苗以酵母菌重組表現）的皮膚測試與 IgE 檢測。

**引句 5（PEG／polysorbate，COVID-19 mRNA 疫苗時期，不建議測試但成分史可換疫苗）**：
> "We recommend against skin testing to PEG, PS or to the mRNA COVID-19 vaccine itself as a means to predict risk of a severe allergic reaction to a COVID-19 vaccine."

出處：AAAAI_ACAAI_COVID疫苗過敏GRADE共識更新_2023.pdf，PDF 第12頁
中文整理：不建議用 PEG、polysorbate（PS）或 mRNA 疫苗本身做皮膚測試來預測發生嚴重過敏反應的風險——此點與 2012年版對明膠/乳膠/新黴素「立即型過敏史者應做皮膚測試」的建議**方向相反**，反映領域在COVID-19後對「測試預測力不足」的重新認識，必須註明是哪個文件、哪個情境（測試無法準確預測 vs. 測試用於釐清已發生反應之成分）。

**引句 6（PEG過敏者可换用不同技術平台疫苗，具體可操作建議）**：
> "If your patient has reported anaphylaxis within minutes of different drugs containing polyethylene glycol (PEG), then this patient should avoid the Covid mRNA vaccines (currently Pfizer and Moderna)... If your patient has a confirmed PEG allergy, but has tolerated an influenza vaccine or other parenteral drug containing polysorbate 80, then the Astra Zeneca vaccine may be administered after ensuring that there are no other contraindications to the AZ vaccine"

出處：BSACI_vaccine_allergy_FAQs_for_GPs_2021.pdf，PDF 第1頁
中文整理：確認PEG過敏（曾對含PEG藥物於數分鐘內發生過敏性休克）者應避開 mRNA 疫苗（Pfizer/Moderna）；若曾耐受含 polysorbate 80 的流感疫苗或其他非經腸藥物，則可考慮改打 AstraZeneca（腺病毒載體）疫苗——**這是本引句地圖中最直接對應使用者「A過敏→B可考慮」需求的一條**，但屬英國2021年COVID疫苗期間文件，需註明時空背景（AZ疫苗現已多國下架，此建議之疫苗選項本身已過時，但「换用不含共同致敏成分之技術平台」的原則仍可參考）。

## (d) 哪些情況指引明文「可在監測下接種／可考慮」與「不建議／禁忌」

**「不建議／禁忌」類：**

**引句 1**：
> "In general, a history of a severe allergic reaction to a vaccine should be considered a contraindication to additional doses of the same vaccine"

出處：CDC 通則 General Best Practice Guidelines for Immunization.pdf，PDF 第76頁（printed 76）
中文整理：原則上，對某疫苗曾有嚴重過敏反應，視為該疫苗再次接種之**禁忌**（注意：此為「原則上」的通則敘述，實際上同一份文件與 AAAAI/WAO 皆進一步說明可透過成分導向評估、皮膚測試、轉介過敏科來突破此原則性禁忌，並非絕對不可接種）。

**引句 2**：
> "A previous severe allergic reaction to influenza vaccine, regardless of the component suspected to be responsible for the reaction, is a contraindication to future receipt of the vaccine"

出處：CDC 通則 General Best Practice Guidelines for Immunization.pdf，PDF 第77頁（printed 77；已用 `pdftotext -f 77 -l 77` 單頁抽取回查確認，footer 頁碼與 PDF 頁序一致為 1:1）
中文整理：曾對流感疫苗本身（而非僅對蛋）發生嚴重過敏反應者，無論懷疑的成分為何，皆為該疫苗未來接種之禁忌。

**「可在監測下接種／可考慮」類：**

**引句 3**：
> "If the skin test results are negative, subsequent doses can be administered in the usual manner but under observation. If the skin test results are positive and the patient requires subsequent doses, the vaccine can be administered in graded doses under observation."

出處：AAAAI_ACAAI疫苗過敏practice_parameter_2012.pdf，PDF 第3頁（印刷頁碼 **27**）
中文整理：皮膚測試陰性者可常規接種但需監測；陽性但仍需接種者，**可考慮**在監測下採分次漸增劑量方式給予。

**引句 4**：
> "Recommendation 1a: For patients with no history of a previous allergic reaction to a COVID-19 vaccine or its excipients, the risk of first-dose COVID-19 vaccine-induced anaphylaxis is exceptionally low, and we recommend vaccination over either no vaccination or vaccine deferral." (Strong Recommendation; High Certainty of Evidence)

出處：AAAAI_ACAAI_COVID疫苗過敏GRADE共識更新_2023.pdf，PDF 第7頁
中文整理：對COVID-19疫苗或其賦形劑無過敏史者，首劑誘發過敏性休克風險極低，**強烈建議接種**優於不接種或延後接種（高證據等級）。

**引句 5（明確可考慮監測下接種的第三類情境）**：
> "接種場所應具備訓練過之醫療人員及適當醫療設備，以即時對接種後發生之立即全身性嚴重過敏反應（anaphylaxis）進行處置。對於疫苗成分有嚴重過敏反應史，或對於先前接種之疫苗劑次發生嚴重過敏反應者，為接種禁忌。"

出處：疾管署_COVID19疫苗接種場所嚴重過敏反應處置建議_2021.pdf，PDF 第1頁
中文整理：台灣疾管署官方立場——對疫苗成分或先前劑次曾有嚴重過敏反應者，列為接種禁忌；同時要求接種場所須具備處理立即型嚴重過敏反應的能力與設備（隱含：符合條件、有妥善監測與急救能力者，仍可在特定情況下評估接種）。此為我國本地官方文件用語，可作為「不建議／禁忌」分類的本土依據，但**未如AAAAI/WAO那樣進一步說明成分導向評估後可考慮接種的細節**，此落差應在網站上誠實標示（我國文件較簡略，細節需引用國際文件補充）。

## 交叉核對重點（供网站作者參考，非引句）

1. **MMR疫苗蛋過敏 vs 明膠過敏是兩件事**：WAO ICON 明確指出 MMR 過敏性休克「most... attributed to gelatin allergy」而非蛋過敏；蛋過敏者可安全接種 MMR。這與流感/黃熱病的蛋過敏考量邏輯不同，三者不可互相類推（呼應使用者 memory 中「同句禁忌在不同疫苗分屬不同欄位不可類推」的既有 GOTCHA 原則）。
2. **皮膚測試的角色因情境而異**：2012年 AAAAI 對「已發生反應後」建議皮膚測試以確認機轉與可疑成分；2023年 GRADE 更新對「未發生反應、接種前」明確不建議测试预测风险，對「PEG/PS」測試本身的預測力也表示不建議（測試結果不可靠）。網站若要引用「建議皮膚測試」務必註明是哪一種情境。
3. **BSACI 引句6是唯一直接命中「A過敏換B疫苗」情境的可操作建議**，但屬2021年COVID疫苗初期、且提及的AZ疫苗現況已改變，使用時應加註時空背景與「原則仍可參考、具體疫苗選項已過時」的但書。
